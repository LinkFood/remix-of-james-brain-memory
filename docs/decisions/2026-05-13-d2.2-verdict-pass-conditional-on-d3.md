The marginal band the threshold-drop was supposed to capture is **empirically empty** for these 3 tickers — in the window AND in the historical baseline.

## Why this is a PASS not a FAIL

Under a **continuous** specialist conviction distribution, an empty 55-59 marginal band over 5 trading days would be structurally surprising — the threshold drop would suggest a signal-capture gap (specialists are firing in the marginal band but the gate is too restrictive, OR specialists rarely reach 55-59 because the conviction calibration is broken). FAIL territory.

Under a **bimodal** specialist conviction distribution — the property surfaced 2026-05-06 via cross-ticker AAPL+NVDA+QQQ analysis — there is no marginal band. Specialists either land in the low cluster (`conv <50`, "waiting, no thesis") or the high cluster (`conv ≥60`, "thesis crystallized, near-flagging"). Reads in the 50-59 band are structurally rare because that's where the bimodal distribution has zero density. Under bimodal, the threshold drop is **operationally harmless** — no reads exist in the marginal band to catch or miss. The threshold's POSITION in 55-65 is structurally irrelevant under bimodal.

The empirical evidence in the D2.2 window is consistent with bimodal. So PASS.

## Why conditional on D3

D3 (`scripts/d3_experiment/` — 14-day feature-isolation window) is the experiment that resolves the bimodal-vs-continuous fork structurally. The empirical D2.2 window (5 days) is suggestive but not statistically decisive on its own. D3's 14-day capture + per-ticker Hartigan dip test + logistic regression provides the structural answer.

**D3 outcome → D2.2 status:**
- **D3 → bimodal**: D2.2 PASS stands. GOOGL/AMZN/META join AAPL/NVDA as hardened.
- **D3 → continuous**: D2.2 RE-OPENS. Empty marginal band over 5 days under continuous distribution is structurally surprising; threshold drop becomes suspicious; D2.2 verdict re-rendered per the surprise.
- **D3 → hybrid**: D2.2 verdict re-rendered per the fork's actual shape.
